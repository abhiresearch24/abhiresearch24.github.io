## Best Technical Interaction Award — IWSEPP 2024

**International Workshop on Sustainable Electrostatic Precipitation (IWSEPP-2024)**  
**2024**

Received the **Best Technical Interaction Award** worth 150 Euros at IWSEPP-2024 in recognition of active technical engagement and scientific discussions during the workshop.

The workshop brought together researchers working on electrostatic precipitation, aerosol science, particle collection, and related technologies.
