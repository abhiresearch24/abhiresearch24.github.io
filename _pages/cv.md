## Research Profile

Researcher working at the intersection of **Scientific Machine Learning, Bayesian Inverse Problems, Uncertainty Quantification, Computational Fluid Dynamics, and Multiphysics Modeling**.

My research combines high-fidelity physics-based simulations with machine learning, deep generative models, explainable artificial intelligence, and optimization to address forward and inverse problems in computational engineering.

My doctoral research focused on charged particle dynamics and electrostatic collection using multiphysics simulations, interpretable machine learning, and surrogate-assisted multi-objective optimization. My current research focuses on **deep learning-based Bayesian inverse problems and uncertainty quantification**.

### Doctoral Researcher
**Indian Institute of Technology Delhi**  
Department of Mechanical Engineering  
*January 2020 – February 2026*

**Ph.D. Supervisors:** Prof. Bahni Ray and Dr. Mayank Kumar

Conducted doctoral research integrating **multiphysics simulations, computational fluid dynamics, electrostatics, Lagrangian particle tracking, machine learning, explainable AI, and multi-objective optimization** for charged aerosol particle collection.

Major research contributions included:

- Development of high-fidelity three-dimensional multiphysics models for charged particle transport.
- Experimental validation of numerical particle collection models.
- Investigation of electrostatic, hydrodynamic, and particle rebound mechanisms.
- Development of machine learning surrogate models for computationally efficient prediction.
- Application of SHAP, PDP, and other explainability methods for identifying dominant physical parameters.
- Development of clustering-guided design-space reduction methodologies.
- Integration of surrogate models with NSGA-II for multi-objective engineering optimization.
